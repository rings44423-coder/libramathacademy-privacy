Libra Math Academy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Libra Math Academy ("the App") is an educational mathematics application developed by Emmanuel Lufumbe. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Libra Math Academy does not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, phone number, or email address to use the main educational features of the App.

The App and its third-party services may automatically process certain technical and device-related information, including device identifiers, app information, diagnostics, crash information, and information necessary to provide notifications and advertisements.

2. Advertising

Libra Math Academy uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to users who are not using premium features. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related information in accordance with their applicable policies and settings.

3. Firebase Services

The App uses Firebase services provided by Google. These services may process technical information, diagnostics, crash information, and information necessary to deliver notifications and maintain the reliability and security of the App.

Firebase Cloud Messaging may use a device-specific registration token to deliver notifications to the App.

4. Purchases and Premium Features

Libra Math Academy may offer optional premium subscriptions through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google Play. The App does not receive or store your full payment card number.

Information associated with a purchase, such as a purchase token, product identifier, and purchase status, may be processed to verify purchases and provide premium access.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the App.
  • Deliver app notifications.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Detect and diagnose technical problems.
  • Verify premium purchases.
  • Protect the App against fraud and unauthorized use.

6. Information Sharing

The App uses third-party service providers, including Google services such as Firebase, Google AdMob, and Google Play services, to operate certain features. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Libra Math Academy is an educational mathematics application. The App does not knowingly require children to provide personal information or create an account.

9. Your Choices

You can control certain advertising and notification permissions through your Android device settings and, where applicable, your Google account settings. Premium features are optional.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
